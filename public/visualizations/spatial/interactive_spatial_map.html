FireScope - interactive 0.05° candidate-activity map (2023-2025)

Read carefully: Satellite thermal detections (type 0) are vegetation-fire CANDIDATES, not confirmed fires. FAI is an index relative to a frozen 2023-2025 reference (v1; 100 = reference average); it is not burned area, fire intensity or a fire count. Gaps = unusable/missing days, never zero activity. Blank cells = no candidate recorded in this product. Counts are raw cell-days on observed days (VIIRS, not coverage-normalised); frequency uses national usable days as denominator.
