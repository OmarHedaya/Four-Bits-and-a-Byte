## Output files

- `firescope_harmonized.csv`: one row per detection, common schema.
- `cell_day_presence.csv`: cross-sensor cells with at least one detection per day.
- `coverage_calendar.csv`: per sensor and day coverage status.
- `harmonization_inputs.csv`: inputs used and hash checks.
