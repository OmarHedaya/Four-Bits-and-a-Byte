FireScope - monthly headline FAI (interactive)

Satellite thermal detections (type 0) are vegetation-fire CANDIDATES, not confirmed fires. FAI is an index relative to a frozen 2023-2025 reference (v1; 100 = reference average); it is not burned area, fire intensity or a fire count. Gaps = unusable/missing days, never zero activity. Months without a published value are shown as gaps, never zero.
